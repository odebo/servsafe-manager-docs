The Time and Temperature Log: What Inspectors Actually Look For
===============================================================

Building a defensible time and temperature log
----------------------------------------------

A time and temperature log is not a compliance decoration. It is the only written evidence that your operation was actively controlling TCS food during the hours nobody was watching.

- Log the thermometer reading, the item, the time, and the initials of the person who took it.
- Record corrective action when a reading is out of range: what you did, when, and who verified it.
- A log with no out-of-range entries for a year is a red flag, not a perfect record.
- Keep logs for the period your jurisdiction requires, typically 90 days to 6 months.

The four numbers the exam tests
-------------------------------

- Cold holding: 41 F (5 C) or lower.
- Hot holding: 135 F (57 C) or higher.
- Cooking poultry, stuffed items, reheated food: 165 F (74 C) for 15 seconds.
- Cooling: 135 to 70 F within 2 hours, then 70 to 41 F within 4 more hours.
