The Three-Compartment Sink: Wash, Rinse, Sanitize
=================================================

The procedure in the order the exam wants it
--------------------------------------------

- Compartment 1: wash in water at least 110 F (43 C) with detergent.
- Compartment 2: rinse in clean water.
- Compartment 3: sanitize, either with a chemical sanitizer at the correct concentration or with hot water at least 171 F (77 C).
- Air-dry everything. Towel drying re-contaminates the surface.

The 2022 Food Code lowered the manual warewashing wash temperature to 110 F, and the exam follows the current edition.
