A 7-Day Study Plan
==================

Day 1 - Domains 1 and 2: management systems, personal hygiene.\nDay 2 - Domain 3: time and temperature control.\nDay 3 - Domain 4: cross-contamination.\nDay 4 - Domain 5: cleaning and sanitizing.\nDay 5 - Domains 6 and 7: regulation, purchasing, receiving, storage.\nDay 6 - Full timed 90-question mock exam.\nDay 7 - Review only your weak domains, then rest.
