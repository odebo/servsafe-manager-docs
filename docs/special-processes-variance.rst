Special Processes: When You Need a Variance
===========================================

ROP, sous vide, smoking, and curing
-----------------------------------

Some processes require a written variance from the regulatory authority before you can do them at all, because the standard Food Code temperatures do not apply.

- Reduced-oxygen packaging (ROP), including vacuum sealing and sous vide.
- Curing and smoking food to preserve it rather than to flavour it.
- Treating food with additives to extend shelf life.
- Sprouting seeds or beans.
- Serving raw or partially cooked animal food, such as raw oysters or rare hamburgers, for which a consumer advisory is required.
