Integrated Pest Management
==========================

Pest control shows up as an exam scenario: the manager finds evidence of pests and must
choose what to do, in order.

The order is always:

1. **Deny entry** — seal cracks, keep doors closed, screen windows and vents.
2. **Deny food and water** — store food in sealed, food-grade containers, off the floor,
   clean up spills immediately, fix leaks.
3. **Deny shelter** — remove clutter, keep storage areas clean, keep dumpsters away from
   the door and covered.
4. **Eliminate** — only then, work with a licensed pest control operator (PCO). You do not
   set traps over food-contact surfaces.

Exam traps: spraying pesticide yourself around food storage is **always wrong**; so is
storing pest-control chemicals next to food.
