ServSafe Manager Passing Score
==============================

The ServSafe Manager exam is scored on a 0-100 scale and **75 is passing**. That number
is the same for every version of the exam, but it is *not* 75% of the questions.

What that means in practice:

- The exam has 90 scored questions (plus a handful of unscored pilot items).
- 75/100 is a scaled score, so a handful of misses is survivable; a run of misses in one
  domain is not, because the exam is blueprint-weighted.
- Failing domains are reported back to you on the score report. That report is the single
  most useful study document you will get all year.

Most people fail on **Domain 3 (time and temperature control)** and **Domain 2 (personal
hygiene)**, not on the obscure regulation items.

Check where you actually stand with a mapped
`580-question practice bank <https://foodsafeprep.com/>`_ — it reports a per-domain
weakness map instead of a single percentage.
