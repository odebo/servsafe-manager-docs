Warewashing Machines: Temperature and Pressure Standards
========================================================

Numbers that are easy to miss
-----------------------------

- High-temperature machines: final rinse at least 180 F (82 C) at the manifold, or 160 F (71 C) at the surface of the dish.
- Low-temperature machines: chemical sanitizer at the correct concentration, per the manufacturer label.
- Water pressure between 15 and 25 psi, enough to remove food but not enough to blow it around the room.
- Scrape, pre-soak or pre-flush, then load items so water reaches every surface.
- Air-dry; never towel-dry.
