ServSafe vs. Prometric vs. other food manager exams
===================================================

Three credentials dominate US food manager certification:

- **ServSafe Food Protection Manager** (National Restaurant Association) — the one most
  chains and most states recognise.
- **Prometric Food Protection Manager Certification** — ANSI-accredited, common in
  grocery and manufacturing.
- **360training / StateFoodSafety / Learn2Serve** — state-approved courses that feed into
  one of the accredited exams.

They are *not* interchangeable for the provider's own course, but the **content overlap is
large**: FDA Food Code, time and temperature control, cross-contamination, cleaning and
sanitising, and a management-systems domain.

Before you buy anything, check which credential your **state or county health department**
accepts, and whether your employer reimburses a specific one.
