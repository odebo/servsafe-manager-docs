Time and Temperature Control
============================

These are the numbers the ServSafe Manager exam expects you to recall without a reference.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Item
     - Temperature
   * - Refrigerator storage
     - 41 F (5 C) or lower
   * - Hot holding
     - 135 F (57 C) or higher
   * - Cold holding
     - 41 F (5 C) or lower
   * - Cooking poultry
     - 165 F (74 C) for 15 seconds
   * - Cooking ground meat
     - 155 F (68 C) for 17 seconds
   * - Cooking fish / whole meat
     - 145 F (63 C) for 15 seconds
   * - Reheating for hot holding
     - 165 F (74 C) for 15 seconds
   * - Danger zone
     - 41 F - 135 F (5 C - 57 C)
