HACCP and Active Managerial Control
====================================

The exam expects you to distinguish two things that sound the same:

- **HACCP** is a written, seven-principle food safety system for a specific process or
  menu item. It is about a plan.
- **Active managerial control** is what a manager does day to day — monitoring, correcting,
  retraining, and documenting — to keep the biggest risks under control. It is about
  behaviour.

The seven HACCP principles: conduct a hazard analysis, determine critical control points,
establish critical limits, establish monitoring procedures, establish corrective actions,
establish verification procedures, establish record-keeping.

For the exam, the practical answer is usually active managerial control: identify the
**five most common risk factors** (purchasing from unapproved sources, inadequate cooking,
improper holding temperatures, contaminated equipment, poor personal hygiene) and control
those first.
