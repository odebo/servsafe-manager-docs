Washing Produce: What the Food Code Requires
============================================

A short rule with common errors
-------------------------------

- Wash raw fruits and vegetables under running water before cutting, cooking, or serving.
- Do not use soap, detergent, or bleach on produce.
- Wash even items with a peel or rind that will not be eaten.
- Cut leafy greens and cut tomatoes are TCS food and must be held at 41 F (5 C) or lower.
- Pre-cut, pre-washed produce labelled ready-to-eat does not need a second wash.
