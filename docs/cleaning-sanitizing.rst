Cleaning and Sanitising
=======================

Domain 5 is short and highly testable. Two numbers do most of the work:

- **Sanitiser concentration** must match the manufacturer's label — too weak is a failure,
  too strong is a chemical contamination risk.
- **Contact time** matters more than concentration. Most failures in the field are people
  wiping a sanitiser off in two seconds.

Three-compartment sink order: wash, rinse, sanitise. Air dry — never towel dry.

For a chlorine sanitiser, the classic exam numbers are **50-99 ppm** for food-contact
surfaces, with the water at least **75 F (24 C)** when the sanitiser is mixed in a sink.

Also worth memorising: **clean before you sanitise**. A sanitiser applied to food soil is
not sanitising anything.
