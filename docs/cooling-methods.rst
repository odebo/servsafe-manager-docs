Cooling Food Safely
===================

Cooling is Domain 3's hardest sub-topic and it is asked as a scenario, not a definition.

The two-stage rule:

1. **135 F to 70 F in 2 hours.**
2. **70 F to 41 F in 4 more hours.**

If the food has not reached 70 F in two hours, you must **reheat it to 165 F for 15
seconds** and start the cooling process again. Reheating resets the clock; it does not
rescue food that has already spent too long warm.

Approved cooling methods:

- Ice-water bath with frequent stirring
- Ice paddle
- Blast chiller
- Small shallow pans, no more than **4 inches** deep, loosely covered
- Cut large items into smaller portions

Never cool at room temperature and never stack hot pans of food together.
