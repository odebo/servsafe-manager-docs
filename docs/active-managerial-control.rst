Active Managerial Control: The Concept Behind the Whole Exam
============================================================

Not a program, a practice
-------------------------

Active managerial control means the manager actively identifies the risks specific to their operation and puts controls in place, rather than relying on a generic plan. It is the conceptual spine of the ServSafe Manager curriculum.

- Identify the foodborne illness risk factors specific to your menu and operation.
- Put written procedures in place for each risk.
- Monitor the procedures, and correct out-of-range results.
- Verify that the controls work, using logs, testing, and observation.
- Retrain when a control fails, not only when an employee is new.
