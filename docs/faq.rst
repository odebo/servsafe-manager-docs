Frequently Asked Questions
==========================

**How long is the ServSafe Manager exam?**
90 scored questions, 2 hours. A 10-question version is used for exam retakes in some
settings.

**Can I take it online?**
Yes — online proctored exams exist, in addition to in-person paper and computer exams.

**What score do I need?**
75 on the 75-100 scaled score. See the passing score page.

**How many questions can I miss?**
It depends on which domains you miss them in. The exam is blueprint-weighted, so misses are
not equally costly.

**Do I need the book?**
The book is not the bottleneck for most retakes. Domain-level drilling is.

**How do I know which domain I am failing?**
Your score report lists per-domain performance. If you never got one, a diagnostic
`practice bank <https://foodsafeprep.com/>`_ with a per-domain weakness map does the
same job in about 40 minutes.
