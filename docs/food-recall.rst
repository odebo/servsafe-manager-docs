Food Recalls: The Manager's Procedure
=====================================

Recall response as a management-system skill
--------------------------------------------

- Identify the recalled product and stop service immediately, including food already plated.
- Segregate and label the product so it cannot be reintroduced by mistake.
- Follow the recall notice: return, destroy, or hold according to the notice instructions.
- Document what was on hand, what was served, and what was removed.
- Notify the supplier and the regulatory authority when required.

Recall items belong to the management-systems domain, which candidates often under-prepare because it feels less practical than handwashing.
