Exam Blueprint
==============

The ServSafe Manager exam is built from a 7-domain, 21-task blueprint. Every question
maps back to one of those tasks, which is why studying by domain beats studying at random.

+----------+---------------------------------------------+
| Domain   | Topic                                       |
+==========+=============================================+
| 1        | Food safety management systems              |
+----------+---------------------------------------------+
| 2        | Good personal hygiene                       |
+----------+---------------------------------------------+
| 3        | Controlling time and temperature            |
+----------+---------------------------------------------+
| 4        | Preventing cross-contamination              |
+----------+---------------------------------------------+
| 5        | Cleaning and sanitizing                     |
+----------+---------------------------------------------+
| 6        | Food safety regulation and standards        |
+----------+---------------------------------------------+
| 7        | Purchasing, receiving, and storage          |
+----------+---------------------------------------------+

A full 580-item bank mapped to all 21 tasks is available at
`FoodSafe Prep <https://foodsafeprep.com/>`_ — with a live weakness map that shows which
domains you are actually failing.

See also `the exam blueprint page <https://foodsafeprep.com/servsafe-manager-exam-blueprint>`_.
