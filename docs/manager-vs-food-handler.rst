Manager Certification vs. Food Handler Card
===========================================

These are two different things and the confusion causes real fines.

- **Food handler card / certificate**: 1-3 hour course, low cost, almost every employee
  needs one. Not a management credential.
- **Food protection manager certification**: the ANSI-accredited exam (ServSafe Manager and
  peers). Usually one certified manager per shift, per facility, depending on your state.
- Some states (California, Texas, Illinois among them) require the manager credential
  statewide; others leave it to the county.

If you are being told you need "the ServSafe thing" for a promotion, ask for the exact
credential name in writing — you may be buying the wrong exam.
