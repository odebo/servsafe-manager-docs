Receiving Temperatures: What to Reject and Why
==============================================

The receiving inspection in exam order
--------------------------------------

- Cold TCS food: 41 F (5 C) or lower.
- Hot TCS food: 135 F (57 C) or higher.
- Milk and shellfish: 45 F (7 C) or lower is acceptable on receipt.
- Frozen food: solidly frozen, no large ice crystals, no frozen liquid at the bottom of the case.
- Eggs: 45 F (7 C) or lower, clean and uncracked.
- Reject any delivery with signs of temperature abuse, pest damage, or damaged packaging.
