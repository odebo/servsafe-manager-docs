Sanitiser Strength and Contact Time
===================================

Domain 5 numbers, exactly as the exam asks them:

- **Chlorine**: 50-99 ppm for food-contact surfaces.
- **Quaternary ammonium (quats)**: 200-400 ppm, or whatever the label states.
- **Iodine**: 12.5-25 ppm.
- Water temperature when mixing a chlorine sanitiser in a sink: at least **75 F (24 C)**.
- Contact time: follow the label; a 7-second wipe is not sanitising.

Three-compartment sink: **wash, rinse, sanitise**, then air dry. Never towel dry — a towel
re-introduces pathogens.

Two rules that generate the most missed questions:

1. **Clean before you sanitise.** A sanitiser on food soil is not effective.
2. **Never mix chemicals.** A sanitiser must be approved and used at label concentration,
   never "a bit stronger to be safe".
