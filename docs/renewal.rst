ServSafe Manager Recertification
=================================

A ServSafe Manager certificate is valid for **5 years** from the date of the exam.

What people get wrong about renewal:

1. There is no "renewal exam" discount and no grandfathering. You retake the same exam.
2. The clock runs from your **certificate date**, not from the day your employer filed it.
3. Some states layer their own food manager requirement on top and track it separately —
   a valid ServSafe certificate does not automatically satisfy a state licence.
4. If your certificate expires while you are working, most jurisdictions treat it as
   working without a certified manager on site.

Practical approach: mark a calendar reminder 90 days before expiry, then do a diagnostic
run rather than reading the book cover to cover. A timed
`practice exam <https://foodsafeprep.com/>`_ shows which domains decayed in five years
of not thinking about them.
