Facility and Equipment Standards
================================

The short, testable list for Domain 6:

- **Floor drains** must be designed so that waste water cannot back up into food areas.
- **Handwashing sinks** must be used only for handwashing — never for food prep or dumping.
- **Food-contact surfaces** must be smooth, non-absorbent, and easy to clean.
- **Light bulbs** must be shielded or shatter-resistant over food and equipment.
- **Ventilation** must carry grease-laden air away from cooking areas; hood filters are
  cleaned on a schedule, not when visibly dirty.
- **Garbage containers** must be leakproof, insect-resistant, and covered. Outside
  containers need a tight-fitting lid.

Anything described in two or more pieces, with cracks, or that cannot be cleaned in place
is the wrong answer on the exam.
