Purchasing, Receiving, and Storage
==================================

Domain 7 is the one experienced cooks skip — and then miss. The exam asks about the
**receiving** step far more than about vendors.

Receiving checks:

- Cold TCS food: **41 F (5 C) or lower**.
- Live shellfish: air temperature **45 F (7 C)** and **internal 50 F (10 C) or lower**.
- Milk: **45 F (7 C) or lower**.
- Frozen food: solid, no evidence of thaw-and-refreeze.
- Reject any delivery with damaged packaging, water stains, or a smell you would not serve.

Storage order, top shelf to bottom: ready-to-eat, seafood, whole cuts of beef and pork,
ground meat, then poultry.

Every one of these items has a drill at
`FoodSafe Prep <https://foodsafeprep.com/>`_.
