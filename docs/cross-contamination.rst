Preventing Cross-Contamination
==============================

This is Domain 4 and it is where scenario questions live. The exam rarely asks "what is
cross-contamination" — it describes a shift and asks what the manager should do first.

The rules worth memorising:

- **Separate**: raw meat stored below ready-to-eat food, never above.
- **Store by cooking temperature**: poultry on the bottom shelf, then ground meat, then
  whole cuts, then seafood, then ready-to-eat.
- **Clean and sanitise**: after a task change, after 4 hours of continuous use, and any
  time the surface touched raw animal product.
- **Dedicate**: separate cutting boards by colour or by label, and never reuse a container
  that held raw animal food for ready-to-eat food.
- **Hands**: bare-hand contact with ready-to-eat food is prohibited unless a specific
  alternative is followed.
