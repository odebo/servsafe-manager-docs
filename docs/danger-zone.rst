The Temperature Danger Zone
===========================

**41 F to 135 F (5 C to 57 C).** That range is the single most examined concept on the
ServSafe Manager exam, and it appears in every domain — not just the temperature one.

Why it matters:

- TCS food left in the danger zone grows bacteria fast. The classic figure is that
  bacteria can double every **20 minutes** in that range.
- The exam measures time in the zone, not temperature alone. Two hours is the point where
  food must be discarded; the four-hour window applies to food that started cold.
- Holding, cooling, reheating, and receiving all express the same rule with different
  numbers attached.

Cooling is the one people fail: **135 F to 70 F within 2 hours**, then **70 F to 41 F
within 4 more hours** — six hours total, and the first two are the hard deadline.
