Thawing Food Safely
===================

Four approved methods, and the exam will offer you a wrong one as a distractor:

1. **Refrigeration** — 41 F or lower, always the safest answer.
2. **Running water** — 70 F or lower, submerged, with enough flow to float the food away.
3. **Microwave** — only if the food goes straight into cooking afterwards.
4. **As part of cooking** — frozen to cooked in one continuous step.

Never thaw on a counter, in a sink of standing water, or in a warm room.

The trap: water temperature. "Warm running water" is always wrong; **70 F (21 C) or
lower** running water is the correct phrasing.
