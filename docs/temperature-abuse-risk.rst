Who is most vulnerable, and what changes because of it
------------------------------------------------------

The exam frames several rules differently for highly susceptible populations, which means the answer changes depending on who you serve.

- Highly susceptible populations include preschool children, elderly people, and people who are immunocompromised.
- In operations serving those groups, the Food Code prohibits bare-hand contact with ready-to-eat food outright.
- No time as a public health control without a written procedure and prior approval.
- Never re-serve food that has already been served to a customer.
